Privacy policy

Tracking App, last updated November 2026.

Tracking App is a personal app that runs only on its owner's own Windows PC. It is used by its owner alone and isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Your Google account's email address, to tell connected accounts apart.
  • Google Calendar: your events (read-only), and creating events only if you allow it.
  • Gmail: your messages, read-only, if you allow it.

Where your data goes

  • Everything stays on your own computer. The app talks only to Google's own servers; it has no server of its own.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your computer. Email bodies are not stored; they're fetched when you open a message.
  • Your data is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or read by anyone else. There is no analytics or tracking.

Tracking App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect the account in Tracking App (Settings), which deletes its tokens and cached data on your PC.
  • Remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling and deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored.
